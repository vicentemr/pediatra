# Surface: pediatrician site + ad landing

Scope: `src/pages/**` (home/profile, courses, booking, legal) and `src/pages/landing.astro` + `src/pages/en/landing.astro`.
Mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: parents deciding to trust, book and pay; or buy a feeding course.
Primary action: book a paid appointment (open slots visible). Secondary: buy a course.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a child-health growth record (cartilla de salud infantil), not a clinic brochure. It refuses the white-and-hospital-blue split hero with a doctor photo and three icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Graph-paper ground in pale sage with a fine green grid; bottle-green fields owning whole bands; percentile curves in tomato and ochre ink; stamp-like labels; Bricolage Grotesque display, Figtree text, tabular numerals for times and prices.
STORY: Parents see a calm record of how children grow, understand that Dr. Mori accompanies each stage (and feeding at each stage), see real next slots, and book or buy.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, headline and booking action; right and behind, a large percentile chart whose curves draw in, with course stages plotted as points on the P50 line. Bottom strip: today's open slots as tappable times.
FORM: Growth chart / child-health booklet, my rank 1 of 7. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
