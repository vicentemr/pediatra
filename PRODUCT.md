# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static site, no database). Booking with paid appointments through Cal.com + Stripe; course sales through Stripe Payment Links. Chosen with the developer for a 300 € budget and zero monthly cost to the client.

## Users

Parents (mostly mothers and fathers of babies and children 0–14) in Málaga, Spain, Spanish- and English-speaking, usually on a phone, often arriving from a Google or Meta ad. They want to know whether this pediatrician is trustworthy, book a visit and pay it, or buy a child-feeding course.

## Product Purpose

The website of a private pediatrician: show his profile and specialties, offer child-feeding courses for sale, and let parents book and pay an appointment online. A separate ad landing page converts paid traffic into appointments, with a course offer as a second path.

## Positioning

A pediatrician who combines general consultation with practical child-feeding education (complementary feeding, BLW, picky eating), so parents can go from "worried about how my child eats or grows" to a booked visit or a course in one place.

## Operating Context

- Appointments are paid in full at booking, card only (no Bizum in this phase).
- Courses are offers only: after payment the pediatrician grants access manually.
- Spanish (default) and English.
- Delivered to the client after build; no ongoing developer maintenance assumed. Both the developer and the pediatrician may edit content.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No database, no backend, no user accounts.
- Booking and payment links are placeholders until the client creates Cal.com and Stripe accounts.
- No domain yet.

## Evidence on Hand

None. Doctor name for the demo: Dr. Vicente Mori, 5 years of experience. All bios, specialties, course titles, prices, address, collegiate number and schedules are demo content and must be replaced. No testimonials, reviews or ratings exist; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Booking is always one tap away and works on a phone.
2. Clear prices before payment; no surprises.
3. Calm medical trust without hospital coldness.
4. Never claim credentials, reviews or results the doctor has not provided.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Many visitors read on small phones, sometimes one-handed with a child in arm.
